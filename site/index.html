HeyReceipt

Hand it your card statement and your receipts. It matches them up, tells you which charges it could not match, and builds a packet you or your bookkeeper can review.

What it does

  • Reads one card statement for one month.
  • Matches each charge to a receipt by vendor, exact amount and date.
  • Never guesses. A charge with no receipt is listed as "Receipt not found".
  • Gives you a packet folder, a zip, and email text you send yourself.

How it gets your receipts

Default (Route B): you save receipt emails into a folder on your own computer. No account is connected and no third party is involved.

An optional free Composio route (Route A) reads your Gmail for you. It is not recommended yet, because some safety questions about it are still unanswered.

Known limits

  • Tried on made-up data only. Not tested on real statements.
  • Works with a CSV export or text pasted from a PDF statement. Please check its parsed lines against your own statement.
  • Bookkeeping only. Ask your preparer anything about taxes.

We do not prepare, file, or advise on tax returns.
